## About Nimbus Forge

Nimbus Forge is a privately held software company founded in **March 2021** by
**Priya Raman** and **Devon Acheng** in **Wellington, New Zealand**. The company
builds **Aurelia**, a real-time data orchestration platform for small robotics
teams.

As of 2026 the company has **142 employees** across three offices: Wellington
(headquarters), Lisbon, and a small support hub in Austin, Texas.

The company name comes from the founders' first prototype, an internal tool
called "the forge", that ran on a cluster they nicknamed "nimbus".

## Mission

Nimbus Forge's stated mission is: *"to give every small robotics team the data
infrastructure that used to require a dedicated platform department."*

## Core values

1. **Ship the boring thing.** Reliability beats novelty.
2. **Default to documentation.** If it isn't written down, it didn't happen.
3. **Customer pain is the roadmap.** Features are prioritised by logged support
   pain, not by internal opinion.
